# Data Release Policy

Aramina is an internal research-draft decision-support project. Full clinical H5
archives and source clinical metadata are controlled inputs and are not tracked
in Git or distributed in reproducibility bundles.

Tracked one-patient prediction fixtures must be approved de-identified or
synthetic examples. They exist only to verify installation, preprocessing, and
prediction contracts. Generated reports, training runs, and large local outputs
are not evidence releases and remain ignored by Git.

Any redistribution of model artifacts, H5 inputs, or report outputs requires
the applicable internal data-governance approval.
